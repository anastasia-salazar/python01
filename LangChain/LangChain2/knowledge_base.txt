Northwind Analytics Employee Handbook (2024)

Office hours: The Pune and Austin offices are open from 8:00 AM to 7:00 PM local time, Monday to Friday.
Work from home: Employees may work from home up to 3 days a week with their manager's approval.
Vacation: Full-time employees receive 22 days of paid vacation per year. Up to 5 unused days can be carried over to the next year.
Sick leave: Employees receive 10 days of paid sick leave per year. A doctor's note is required for absences longer than 3 consecutive days.
IT support: The IT help desk can be reached at extension 4357 or through the #it-help channel. Laptops are replaced every 3 years.
Training: Each employee has an annual learning budget of 1,500 US dollars for courses, books and conferences.
