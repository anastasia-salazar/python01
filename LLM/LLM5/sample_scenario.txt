A 120-person software consultancy is considering replacing its human first-line IT support desk with an AI chatbot. The support desk currently has 6 staff who handle about 900 tickets a month, mostly password resets, VPN problems, laptop setup and software installation requests. Leadership expects the chatbot to resolve 70% of tickets automatically and wants to reassign 4 of the 6 support staff to client projects within three months. The chatbot would need access to the company's identity management system to reset passwords and unlock accounts. Employees work across three time zones, and some client projects handle sensitive healthcare data. Should the company go ahead with this plan, and if so, how should it be rolled out?
